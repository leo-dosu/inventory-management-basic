Order Fulfillment & Logistics

* Order Processing: Allows staff to process and prepare customer orders.
* Order Tracking: Enables staff and customers to track orders from pending to delivered.
* Picking & Packing: Helps warehouse staff select and package ordered products.
* Shipping Management: Manages shipping details and delivery methods.
* Delivery Assignment: Assigns orders to delivery personnel.
* Returns Management: Records and manages returned or exchanged products.
