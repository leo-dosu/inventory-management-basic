\# Feature 1: Inventory Valuation



Inventory Valuation allows the system to determine the monetary value of products held in inventory. It helps businesses understand the financial value of their available stock and supports inventory-related financial planning.



\## Key Functions



\- Calculate the value of inventory.

\- Maintain records of inventory values.

\- Support different inventory valuation methods.

\- Provide information about the financial value of available stock.

\- Help users monitor changes in inventory value.

\- Support inventory planning and financial decision-making.



\# Feature 2: Demand Forecasting



Demand Forecasting uses historical inventory and sales information to estimate future demand for products. It helps businesses plan their inventory levels based on expected future requirements.



\## Key Functions



\- Analyze historical demand and sales information.

\- Estimate future product demand.

\- Identify potential changes in demand patterns.

\- Support inventory planning.

\- Help reduce the risk of excessive or insufficient stock.

\- Assist users in making informed inventory decisions.
