Feature 1:Inventory Optimization

Inventory optimization adds sophistication to a basic inventory plan. Instead of standard ordering formulas and a basic ordering process, it uses tools that provide automated reports, inventory trends, and a view of changes across the entire supply chain, so supply and demand match more closely.

Functions

-Generate automated inventory reports

-Track inventory trends over time

-Give visibility into changes across the entire supply chain

-Support demand-based planning beyond standard ordering formulas

Benefits

-Closer supply-demand match: stock reflects what customers actually need

-Optimized on-hand inventory: less excess, fewer shortages

-Smarter planning: data replaces guesswork and basic formulas

-Supply chain visibility: changes are seen early enough to act

Feature 2: Inventory Alerts

Inventory alerts are triggered by various modules to help reduce waste, optimize inventory financials, and manage customer expectations. They notify you of low stock and of shipment or supply chain delays.

Functions

-Send automated email or SMS alerts for low inventory levels

-Notify you of shipment and supply chain delays

-Support sales forecasting, materials planning, shipping logistics, and supplier management

Benefits

-Less waste: problems are caught before stock is lost or overordered

-Better inventory financials: stock levels stay optimized

-Managed customer expectations: delays are known and communicated early

-Operational control: timely alerts across planning, shipping, and suppliers
