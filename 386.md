\# Feature 1: Goods Receiving Management



Goods Receiving Management allows the system to record and manage products when they are delivered and received into inventory. It helps ensure that incoming goods are properly documented and added to the correct inventory records.



\## Key Functions



\- Record incoming goods and quantities.

\- Verify received products against expected deliveries.

\- Update inventory quantities when goods are received.

\- Record receiving dates and relevant delivery information.

\- Identify missing, damaged, or incorrect items.

\- Maintain records of received inventory.



\# Feature 2:User Roles \& Permissions



User Roles \& Permissions allow the system to control what different users can access and perform within the inventory management software. It helps ensure that users only have access to functions appropriate to their responsibilities.



\## Key Functions



\- Create different user roles.

\- Assign permissions to users.

\- Control access to inventory functions.

\- Restrict sensitive operations to authorized users.

\- Update or remove user permissions when necessary.

\- Support secure management of inventory activities.
