# Dead Stock and Slow-Moving Stock Identification

This feature identifies products that have not sold for a long time or sell very slowly, and lists them for the manager to review.

## Benefits

- Shows which products are tying up money and shelf space.
- Helps managers decide on discounts, promotions or clearance sales.
- Prevents over-ordering of items that do not sell.
- Reduces losses from expired or outdated products.
