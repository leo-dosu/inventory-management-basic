Barcode Scanning

Explanation
Barcode scanning lets you identify products and record stock movements by scanning labels instead of typing details, so inventory data is captured quickly and accurately.

Functions

-Scan barcodes to identify products and locations

-Record receiving, picking, and transfers by scan

-Update inventory levels in real time after each scan

-Generate and print barcode labels

Benefits

-Fewer errors: no manual data entry mistakes

-Speed: faster receiving, picking, and counting

-Real-time accuracy: stock updates as items are scanned

Traceability: every scanned movement is recorded

Cross-Docking

Cross-docking moves incoming goods straight to outbound shipments with little or no storage in between, so products spend less time in the warehouse.

Functions

-Match incoming shipments to open customer orders

-Route received goods directly to outbound docks

-Skip putaway and storage for matched items

-Update inventory and order status automatically

Benefits

-Faster fulfillment: goods reach customers sooner

-Lower storage costs: less warehouse space and handling

-Reduced handling: fewer touches per item

-Better cash flow: stock moves quickly instead of sitting idle
