#Features

## 1. Order Processing
- **Create Order:** Accept customer orders with item details, quantity, and delivery address.
- **Order Validation:** Check if items are in stock before confirming.
- **Order Status Tracking:** Track orders from `Pending` → `Processing` → `Shipped` → `Delivered`.

## 2. Inventory Check & Allocation
- **Stock Availability Check:** Verify stock levels before fulfillment.
- **Auto-Allocation:** Reserve items for an order once confirmed.
- **Backorder Handling:** Flag out-of-stock items and notify the customer.
