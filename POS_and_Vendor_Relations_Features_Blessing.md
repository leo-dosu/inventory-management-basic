## Low-Stock Alerts

The Low-Stock Alerts feature helps the business monitor products that are running low in inventory. It notifies staff when the quantity of a product reaches or falls below its minimum stock level.

### Key Functions

- Monitor the available quantity of each product.
- Set a minimum stock level for products.
- Display a low-stock alert when the quantity becomes low.
- Show the product name and current stock quantity.
- Help staff identify products that need to be restocked.
- Reduce the risk of products becoming completely unavailable.
- Support better inventory planning and restocking decisions.

### Example

| Product | Current Stock | Minimum Stock | Status |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Body Wave Wig | 3 | 5 | Low Stock |
| Bone Straight Wig | 12 | 5 | In Stock |
| Water Wave Wig | 2 | 4 | Low Stock |

> **Note:** A product is considered low in stock when its current quantity is at or below the defined minimum stock level.

### Benefits

- Prevents unexpected stock shortages.
- Makes inventory monitoring easier.
- Helps staff know which products require attention.
- Improves the restocking process.
- Helps maintain product availability.

##. Product Price Management and Price History

The Product Price Management and Price History feature allows authorized users to add, update, and monitor product prices. It also keeps a record of previous prices so that changes can be reviewed when necessary.

### Key Functions

- Add prices to products.
- Update the current selling price.
- Display the current price of each product.
- Record previous prices when changes are made.
- Store the date of each price change.
- Keep track of price changes over time.
- Help staff use accurate prices during sales and checkout.
- Provide a clear history of previous product prices.

### Example

| Product | Previous Price | Current Price | Date Updated |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Body Wave Wig | ₦80,000 | ₦85,000 | 29/09/2026 |
| Bone Straight Wig | ₦90,000 | ₦95,000 | 29/09/2026 |
| Water Wave Wig | ₦70,000 | ₦75,000 | 29/09/2026 |

### Price History

Each price change can contain the following information:

- **Product Name**
- **Previous Price**
- **New Price**
- **Date of Change**
- **User Who Made the Change**
- **Reason for the Change**

### Benefits

- Keeps product prices organized.
- Makes price changes easier to track.
- Reduces pricing errors during checkout.
- Provides a record of previous prices.
- Helps management review pricing changes.
- Supports accurate product and sales management.
