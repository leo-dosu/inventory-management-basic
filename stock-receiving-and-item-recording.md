# Stock Receiving and Item Recording

**Matric Number:** F/ND/25/3210168  
**Group:** Group 1  
**Topic:** Item Identification (SKU Management) and Core Store Keeping

## Feature Name
Stock Receiving and Item Recording

## Short Description
Stock Receiving and Item Recording is the process of receiving goods delivered to a store and recording them accurately in the inventory system. It ensures that newly received items are properly identified and added to the store's stock records.

## Purpose
The purpose is to ensure that all incoming goods are checked, identified, and recorded correctly so that inventory records accurately reflect the actual stock available in the store.

## Main Functions/Activities
- Receive goods from suppliers.
- Check received items against delivery documents.
- Identify items using their SKU or other identification details.
- Record the quantity received.
- Check items for damage or discrepancies.
- Add received quantities to existing stock records.
- Record the date of receipt and relevant supplier information.
- Report missing, damaged, or incorrect items.
