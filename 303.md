\# Inventory Management Software



\## Feature 18: Expiry-Date Tracking



Expiry-Date Tracking allows the inventory management system to record and monitor the expiration dates of products. The system can help users identify products that are approaching their expiry dates.



\### Benefits



\- Helps prevent expired products from being sold or used.

\- Allows users to identify products that need attention.

\- Makes it easier to manage products with limited shelf lives.

\- Helps reduce losses caused by expired inventory.

\- Improves inventory organization and monitoring.

\## Feature 26: Purchase History



Purchase History keeps a record of products previously purchased by the business. It can contain information such as the product purchased, quantity, supplier, purchase date, and purchase cost.



\### Benefits



\- Makes previous purchases easy to review.

\- Helps users monitor purchasing patterns.

\- Makes it easier to track suppliers and previous orders.

\- Helps with budgeting and purchasing decisions.

\- Provides a historical record for inventory management and reporting.
