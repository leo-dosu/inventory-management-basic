\# Feature 1: Advanced Inventory Search \& Filtering



Advanced Inventory Search \& Filtering allows users to quickly find specific inventory items using different search criteria. It makes it easier to locate products within large inventory records.



\## Key Functions



\- Search for products using keywords or identifiers.

\- Filter inventory by category, status, or other criteria.

\- Quickly locate specific inventory items.

\- Support multiple search and filtering conditions.

\- Improve the efficiency of inventory management.

\- Display relevant search results in an organized manner.



\# Feature 2: Inventory Audit Trail



Inventory Audit Trail keeps a chronological record of important actions and changes made to inventory records. It helps users track who performed an action, what was changed, and when the change occurred.



\## Key Functions



\- Record changes made to inventory records.

\- Track users who perform inventory activities.

\- Record the date and time of inventory actions.

\- Maintain a history of inventory changes.

\- Support investigation of unexpected changes or discrepancies.

\- Improve accountability and transparency in inventory management.
