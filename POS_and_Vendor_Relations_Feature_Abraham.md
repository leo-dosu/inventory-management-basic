 1. Product Categories and Variants

- Description:This feature allows the POS system to organize products into categories and manage different variations of the same product, such as size, color, or model.

- Key Functionality:
  - Create and manage product categories.
  - Add different variants of a product.
  - Assign unique SKUs, prices, and stock quantities to variants.
  - Search and filter products by category or variant.

- Relevance:This feature helps businesses organize their inventory, manage different product options, and make product selection easier during sales.

2. Purchase Order Creation and Approval Workflow

- Description: This feature allows a business to create purchase orders when it needs to buy goods from vendors. The purchase order can be reviewed and approved by an authorized manager before it is sent to the vendor.

- Key Functionality:
  - Create purchase orders containing products, quantities, and prices.
  - Select the vendor supplying the products.
  - Submit purchase orders for approval.
  - Allow authorized managers to approve or reject purchase orders.
  - Record approved purchase orders for tracking and future reference.

- Relevance:This feature helps businesses control purchasing activities, prevent unauthorized orders, and maintain proper records of goods ordered from vendors
