Function 1: Machine Learning and AI


AI-powered virtual agents (chatbots) are changing how businesses share inventory availability with ecommerce customers. Using machine learning algorithms and web-based services, they also improve internal inventory controls and forecasting by analyzing real-time data and surfacing trends that are hard to track manually.

Functions

-Communicate out-of-stock inventory to ecommerce customers

-Recommend products based on an active shopping cart or user history

-Automate backorder fulfillment

-Report sales data trends

-Improve forecasting with real-time data analysis

-Report inventory trends that are difficult to track and predict manually

Benefits

-Better customer engagement: sales trend insights help optimize online engagement metrics

-Fewer shortages: stronger internal inventory controls

-More accurate forecasting: decisions rely on real-time data

-Less manual effort: backorders and customer updates are automated

-Higher sales potential: personalized recommendations at the point of purchase

Function 2: Digital Labeling

Digital labeling introduced features like GPS-enabled RFID tags and digital barcoding. With digital inventory data, organizations can network handheld devices with databases for real-time tracking and paperless documentation.

Functions

-Use GPS-enabled RFID tags and digital barcoding to identify items

-Network handheld devices with inventory databases

-Track inventory in real time

-Keep paperless documentation

Benefits

-Real-time visibility: items are tracked as they move

-Paperless operations: less printing, filing, and paperwork

-Accuracy: digital records reduce manual entry errors

-Connected workflows: handheld devices and databases stay in sync
