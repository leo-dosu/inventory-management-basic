\# Feature 1: Inventory Dashboard



An Inventory Dashboard provides users with a centralized view of important inventory information. It allows users to quickly monitor stock levels, inventory status, and other key inventory activities from a single interface.



\## Key Functions



\- Display current inventory levels.

\- Show important inventory information and statistics.

\- Provide an overview of inventory status.

\- Display recent inventory activities.

\- Help users quickly identify important inventory conditions.

\- Present inventory information in an organized and easy-to-understand format.



\# Feature 2: Dead Stock Identification



Dead Stock Identification allows the system to identify products that have remained in inventory for a long period without being sold or used. It helps businesses recognize slow-moving or inactive stock and take appropriate action to reduce unnecessary inventory costs.



\## Key Functions



\- Identify products with little or no movement.

\- Track how long products have remained inactive.

\- Highlight slow-moving inventory.

\- Help users identify stock that may require special attention.

\- Support decisions about reducing or clearing inactive inventory.

\- Help minimize losses associated with obsolete or unused stock.
