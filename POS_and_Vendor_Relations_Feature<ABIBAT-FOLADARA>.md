# Feature Updates: POS and Vendor Relations System

## Feature 1: Inventory and Stock Management

- **Description:**  
  This feature allows the POS system to keep track of products and their available quantities. It helps the business know which products are in stock, which products are running low, and which products need to be restocked.

- **Key Functionality:**
  - Add and update product information.
  - Track the quantity of products available.
  - Automatically reduce stock when a product is sold.
  - Show low-stock alerts.
  - Record products received from vendors.
  - Keep track of stock movement.
  - Help prevent products from running out.

- **Relevance to the System:**  
  This feature helps the business maintain accurate stock records. It also connects the POS system with vendor relations because low-stock products may need to be reordered from vendors.


## Feature 2: Vendor Management

- **Description:**  
  This feature allows the business to store and manage information about its vendors or suppliers in one place.

- **Key Functionality:**
  - Add and update vendor information.
  - Store vendor names and contact details.
  - Record products supplied by each vendor.
  - Track purchases made from vendors.
  - Record outstanding payments to vendors.
  - Keep a history of vendor transactions.
  - Identify which vendor supplies a particular product.

- **Relevance to the System:**  
  This feature makes it easier for the business to communicate with vendors and keep track of purchases and payments. It helps the business maintain organized vendor records.
