Feature 1: Drop Shipping Support

Drop shipping support lets you sell products without holding them in your own stock. When a customer orders, the order goes to the supplier, who ships directly to the customer, and the system tracks it from start to finish.

Functions

-Route customer orders automatically to the right supplier

-Track drop-ship orders and shipment status

-Sync supplier stock availability with your listings

-Record costs and margins for drop-shipped items

Benefits

-Lower overhead: no need to store or handle the goods

-Wider catalog: sell more products without buying stock upfront

-Less manual work: orders flow to suppliers automatically

-Fewer overselling issues: listings reflect supplier availability

Feature 2: Shipping Carrier Integration

Shipping carrier integration connects the system to carriers such as FedEx, UPS, and DHL through API connections. Shipping tasks are handled inside the inventory system, and shipment data syncs automatically.

Functions

-Compare and select carrier rates

-Generate shipping labels

-Track shipments in real time

-Update order and inventory status automatically after dispatch

Benefits

-Time savings: labels and rates come from one system

-Lower shipping costs: easy rate comparison across carriers

-Customer visibility: tracking details are available right away

-Fewer errors: no re-typing addresses or tracking numbers
