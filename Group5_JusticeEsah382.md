#Features
1. Intelligent Route Planning
The system automatically organizes delivery routes based on customer locations, delivery priority, and distance. It can help a delivery partner handle multiple orders in an efficient sequence, reducing unnecessary travel and delivery delays.
Example: If a rider has 5 deliveries around different parts of Lagos, the system can arrange the stops into a practical delivery sequence instead of leaving the rider to decide manually.
2. Multi-Partner Delivery Management
The system allows a business to manage multiple delivery partners simultaneously. Orders can be assigned to different riders or logistics companies, while the system tracks who is responsible for each order, their delivery status, and completed deliveries.
Example: Order #1025 can be assigned to Rider A, while Order #1026 is assigned to a logistics company, with both being tracked from dispatch to delivery.
