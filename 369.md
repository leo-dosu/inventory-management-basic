Feature 1: Stock Discrepancy Flagging

Stock discrepancy flagging automatically compares recorded inventory against actual counts and alerts you when the numbers don't match, so errors are caught and investigated early.

Functions

-Compare system stock levels against physical counts

-Flag mismatches automatically

-Alert relevant users when a discrepancy is detected

-Log discrepancies for review and investigation

Benefits

-Early detection: problems are caught before they grow

-Loss prevention: helps identify theft, damage, or misplaced stock

-Accuracy: records stay aligned with what's on the shelf

-Accountability: a clear record of every discrepancy

Feature 2: Automatic Restock Requests

Automatic restock requests trigger reorders when stock falls to a set threshold, so products are replenished without manual monitoring.

Functions

-Monitor stock levels continuously

-Trigger a restock request when an item reaches its reorder point

-Send requests to suppliers or purchasing staff

-Track the status of pending requests

Benefits

-Fewer stockouts: items are reordered before they run out

-Less manual work: no need to check levels by hand

-Lower overstock: reorders are based on set thresholds

-Faster replenishment: requests go out the moment they're needed
