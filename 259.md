\# Feature 1: SKU Management



SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) Management allows the system to assign and manage unique identification codes for individual products. It helps users easily identify, organize, search, and track products within the inventory.



\## Key Functions



\- Create and assign unique SKUs to products.

\- Search and identify products using SKU codes.

\- Update SKU information when necessary.

\- Associate SKUs with specific product details.

\- Prevent duplicate SKU identification.

\- Improve inventory organization and tracking.



\# Feature 2:ABC Inventory Classification



ABC Inventory Classification organizes inventory items into categories based on their value or importance to the business. It helps users prioritize inventory control and focus more attention on high-value products.



\## Categories



\- \*\*A:\*\* High-value items that require close monitoring.

\- \*\*B:\*\* Medium-value items that require moderate monitoring.

\- \*\*C:\*\* Lower-value items that require less intensive monitoring.



\## Key Functions



\- Classify inventory into A, B, and C categories.

\- Identify high-value inventory items.

\- Prioritize inventory monitoring.

\- Support better inventory control and planning.

\- Help allocate management attention according to product importance.
