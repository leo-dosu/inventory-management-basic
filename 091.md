Point-of-Sale (POS) Integration

POS integration keeps your sales and inventory data constantly in sync. When a customer makes a purchase, the software automatically deducts the sold item from stock levels, so your data stays up to date.

Functions

-Connect sales and inventory data in real time

-Automatically deduct sold items from stock after each purchase

-Keep stock levels current without manual updates

Benefits

-Real-time accuracy: stock levels always reflect the latest sales

-Less manual work: no need to adjust inventory by hand

-Fewer errors: reduces overselling and stock mismatches

-Consistent data: sales and inventory records always agree

Centralized Supplier Management


Centralized supplier management keeps all your supplier information in one place. You can store and manage contact details, track supplier performance, and collaborate with suppliers electronically.

Functions

-Store and manage supplier contact details

-Track supplier performance metrics

-Collaborate with suppliers electronically

Benefits

-Single source of truth: all supplier information in one location

-Better supplier decisions: performance metrics show who is reliable

-Faster communication: electronic collaboration replaces slow back-and-forth

-Stronger relationships: clear records and smoother coordination
