## Feature 1: Purchase Order Creation and Approval Workflow

- **Description:** A system that allows staff to create purchase orders for vendors and send them through an approval process before the order is confirmed.

- **Key Functionality:**
  - Staff can create purchase orders by selecting products, quantities, prices, and vendors.
  - Managers can review and approve or reject purchase orders.
  - The system records the status of each purchase order.
  - Approved orders can be sent to the selected vendor for processing.
  - **Relevance:** Reduces errors in purchasing, prevents unauthorized orders, and provides a clear record of purchases made from vendors.

## Feature 2: Goods Received Notes Matched Against Purchase Orders

- **Description:** A feature that records goods delivered by vendors and compares the received items with the original purchase order.

- **Key Functionality:**
  - Staff can record the products and quantities received from a vendor.
  - The system compares received goods with the quantities ordered.
  - It identifies missing, excess, or incorrect items.
  - Staff can confirm when an order has been completely received.
  - **Relevance:** Helps prevent inventory errors, confirms that vendors deliver what was ordered, and provides accurate records for stock management and payment.
