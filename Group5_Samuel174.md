#features
Product Management: It allows admins and staff to add, update, view, and manage products in the system.
Sales Management: It allows staff to record sales by selecting products and specifying the quantity purchased.
Order Creation: It allows customers and admins to create new purchase orders by selecting products and specifying the required quantity.
Order Tracking: It allows staff and customers to track the current status of an order, such as Pending, Processing, Shipped, Out for Delivery, and Delivered.
Vendor Management: It allows admins to manage vendor information, including vendor names, contact details, products supplied, and transactions.
