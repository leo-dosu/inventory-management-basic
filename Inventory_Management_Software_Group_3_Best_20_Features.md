# Inventory Management Software

## Group 3 — Consolidated Features

### 1. Product/Item Management

Allows users to create, update, organize, and maintain information about products and inventory items.

### 2. SKU Management

Allows the system to assign and manage unique Stock Keeping Units for identifying and organizing products.

### 3. Barcode Scanning

Allows products to be identified and tracked using barcode scanning during inventory operations.

### 4. Batch/Lot Tracking

Tracks inventory according to batch or lot numbers, allowing specific groups of products to be identified throughout their lifecycle.

### 5. Expiry Date Tracking & Warnings

Tracks product expiry dates and provides warnings for products approaching or reaching their expiration dates.

### 6. Serial Number Tracking

Maintains unique serial numbers for individual products to support identification and traceability.

### 7. Stock Transfer Management

Allows inventory to be transferred between locations while recording quantities, source locations, destination locations, and transfer history.

### 8. Multi-Location/Warehouse Management

Allows businesses to manage inventory across multiple warehouses, branches, stores, or other locations.

### 9. Supplier Management

Maintains supplier information and supports the management of supplier-related inventory information.

### 10. Goods Receiving Management

Records and manages products when they are delivered and received into inventory.

### 11. Purchase History

Maintains records of previous inventory purchases, including products, quantities, suppliers, and transaction history.

### 12. Point-of-Sale (POS) Integration

Connects inventory management with point-of-sale systems so that sales can automatically update available stock.

### 13. Inventory Dashboard

Provides a centralized overview of stock levels, inventory status, activities, and important inventory information.

### 14. Inventory Optimization

Helps maintain appropriate inventory levels by balancing product availability, storage requirements, and inventory needs.

### 15. Inventory Alerts

Provides notifications when important inventory conditions occur, such as low or critical stock levels.

### 16. Smart Reordering & Restock Requests

Identifies when inventory needs replenishment and supports the creation or initiation of restock requests.

### 17. Stock Discrepancy Flagging

Identifies differences between recorded inventory quantities and actual physical stock.

### 18. Inventory Valuation

Determines the monetary value of inventory and provides information about the financial value of available stock.

### 19. Demand Forecasting

Uses historical inventory and sales information to estimate future demand for products and support inventory planning.

### 20. Inventory Audit Trail

Maintains a chronological record of important inventory changes and activities, including information about actions performed by users.
