TWENTY FEATURES OF ITEM IDENTIFICTION (SKU MANAGEMENT) AND CORE STORE KEEPING

Item Identification (SKU Management) is the process of assigning a unique Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) code to each distinct product or product variant and maintaining its key details in a central item master, so every item can be clearly identified, tracked, and managed in inventory 

Core Store Keeping is the operational management of physical stock as it moves into and within the store, including activities such as receiving and inspecting goods, recording them by SKU, storing them properly, and issuing them while maintaining accurate stock records.

The Features of Item Identification (SKU Management) Core Store Keeping Features Include;

1  Unique Identification for Each Product

Description:

The system assigns a unique Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) to every distinct product so that each item can be clearly identified and tracked in inventory. 

During item setup, key attributes such as category, brand, size, colour, model, and packaging are captured and used to generate or validate a unique SKU code. 

The system enforces a rule that no two products can share the same SKU and stores all item details in a central item master record keyed by that SKU, ensuring that different versions of a product (for example, different sizes or colours) are treated as separate items.

Purpose:

To eliminate confusion between similar products, improve the accuracy of stock records, and enable precise tracking, reporting, and reordering by SKU.

2   Receiving and Inspection

Description:

This feature handles the process of accepting incoming goods, checking them against the purchase order, and recording valid stock in the inventory system. 

When a delivery arrives, the system supports matching the received items and quantities to the purchase order, inspecting goods for damage, expiry, or incorrect specifications, and noting any discrepancies such as shortages, overages, or defects. 

Once inspection is complete, accepted quantities are recorded in the inventory by SKU and a receipt/GRN is generated, while any issues are flagged for follow‑up with purchasing or suppliers.

Purpose:

To ensure that only correct, good‑quality items enter inventory, that stock levels are accurate from the point of receipt, and that there is a clear record of what was received and in what condition.

3  Stock Receiving and Recording

 Description:
 
Stock Receiving and Recording is a core store-keeping feature used when new goods are delivered to the store. It involves checking the items received and recording them in the inventory so that the available stock quantity remains accurate.

Purpose

The purpose of this feature is to ensure that all goods received by the store are properly checked, identified, and recorded. It helps maintain accurate inventory records and reduces errors in stock quantities.

Main Functions/Activities

* Receive goods from suppliers.
* Check the quantity of goods delivered.
* Identify products using their SKU or item code.
* Compare received goods with the supplier’s information or delivery details.
* Check products for damage or missing items.
* Record newly received products in the inventory.
* Increase the available stock quantity after receiving goods.
* Record important information such as the date of receipt and quantity received.
* Report incorrect, damaged, or missing items when necessary

4  Multi-Zone Bin-Location & Dynamic Slotting Tracker

Description

A core storekeeping module that maps out physical warehouse architecture into hierarchical zones, aisles, racks, shelves, and specific bins, linking every inventory item to an exact physical coordinate.

Purpose

To drastically reduce item retrieval time during stocking and fulfillment, optimize physical storage space utilization, and streamline cycle counts and stock audits.

 Main Functions/Activities
 
- **Warehouse Grid Mapping:** Creates a digital blueprint of the store or warehouse layout categorized by zones and storage tiers.
- **Real-Time Item-to-Bin Assignment:** Associates scanned items directly to specific bin coordinates upon receipt.
- **Bin Transfer & Relocation Logging:** Tracks item movements when inventory is shifted from backroom storage to front shelves or between bins.
- **Storage Capacity Threshold Alerts:** Triggers notifications when specific bins approach maximum volume or weight limits.


 5 Cycle Counting and Stock Reconciliation

 Description

Cycle Counting and Stock Reconciliation is a feature that allows an inventory system to regularly verify the quantity of selected items against the quantities recorded in the system. Instead of counting every item at once, small groups of items are counted on a scheduled basis and any differences are investigated and corrected.

 Purpose

The purpose of this feature is to maintain accurate inventory records and identify discrepancies between physical stock and the quantities recorded by the inventory management system.

It helps reduce errors caused by misplaced items, incorrect stock entries, damage, theft, receiving errors, or other inventory-related mistakes.

 Main Functions/Activities

- Select items or SKUs for scheduled cycle counts.
- Display the expected quantity of each selected item.
- Record the physically counted quantity.
- Compare physical quantities with system quantities.
- Identify and highlight quantity discrepancies.
- Recount items when a discrepancy is detected.
- Record reasons for inventory differences where applicable.
- Adjust inventory records after discrepancies have been verified.
- Maintain a history of previous stock counts and adjustments.
- Schedule future cycle counts for selected items.
- Generate inventory accuracy information from completed counts.

For example, a T-shirt can have different variants such as Small/Blue, Medium/Blue, and Large/Blue. Each variant can be tracked separately while remaining associated with the main product.

 Purpose

The purpose of this feature is to make it easier to identify and organize products that have multiple variations.

It prevents confusion between similar items and allows the inventory system to track the availability and stock quantity of each variant accurately.

Main Functions/Activities

- Create a main item or product record.
- Define attributes such as size, colour, model, or material.
- Create individual variants from the defined attributes.
- Assign a unique identifier or SKU to each variant where required.
- Record stock quantities for individual variants.
- Search and identify products by their attributes.
- View availability for each variant.
- Update variant information when product details change.
- Group related variants under the same main product.
- Generate reports showing stock or availability by variant.
- Maintain separate inventory information for different product variations.

Description: Allows parent products to house complex, multi-tiered variations under a single dashboard.

How it works: Instead of manually inputting 50 separate forms for 50 variations of a shoe, a user creates one parent product ("Air Max 90") and inputs options (5 colors, 10 sizes). The system instantly expands this matrix, creating all 50 distinct SKU profiles automatically.

purpose: Drastically reduces data entry times from hours to seconds for complex retail catalogs.

Main Functions:

Create a parent product with multiple attributes.

Automatically generate SKU combinations from selected attributes.

Assign unique SKU codes to each variation.

Allow users to edit individual variations after generation.

Manage stock, price, and other details for each SKU.

Activities:

Create a parent product, e.g., Air Max 90.

Add attributes such as Color and Size.

Enter the available options, e.g., 5 colors and 10 sizes.

System automatically generates all 50 possible combinations.

System creates a unique SKU profile for each combination.

User reviews or edits the generated SKU details.

User saves the product and its variations to the inventory.

9 Universal History Log

Description: A complete audit trail tracking changes made to product attributes.

How it works: If a manager updates a product's name, brand definition, or weight metric, the system logs the exact timestamp, the user ID who performed the action, and a "before-and-after" snapshot of the data.

purpose: Essential for internal accountability, identifying who made data errors, and recovering lost information.

Main Functions:
Record every change made to product information.

Store the date and exact time of each change.

Identify the user who made the change.

Display before-and-after values.

Allow authorized users to review previous changes.

Support recovery or correction of incorrect product information.

Activities:
A user opens a product record.

The user changes an attribute, such as the product name or weight.

System automatically records the change.

System stores the user's ID and timestamp.

System records the before and after values.

A manager views the product's history log.

The manager identifies who made a particular change and when it occurred.

shirt in different sizes/colors) and handle items bought, stored, and
sold in different units (e.g., box, dozen, piece).

## Purpose
To avoid duplicate product records and prevent counting errors when the
same item is measured in different units.

## Main Functions/Activities

- Assign each variant its own SKU, price, and stock count
- Define base unit of measure (e.g., piece)
- Define conversions (1 box = 12 pieces)
- Convert units automatically when receiving or selling stock
- Display total stock across all variants of a product

14  SKU Generation and Item Master Management

Description

This feature creates a unique Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) code for every product the business sells or stores, and keeps one central record (the "item master") holding all the details of each product. Every other part of the system (stock counts, sales, purchases) refers back to this record.

Purpose

Give every product one unique, permanent identity so no two items are confused.
Remove duplicate or inconsistent product entries.
Make items easy to find, count, sell and reorder.
Provide one trusted source of product information for the whole system.

Main Functions / Activities

Automatically generate a unique SKU for each new item (for example, a code built from category, brand, size and colour).

Allow a manual SKU to be entered, and check that it is not already in use.

Store item details: name, description, category, brand, unit of measure, cost price, selling price, supplier, and reorder level.

Handle product variants (size, colour, model) as separate SKUs linked to one parent product.

Attach barcode or QR code numbers to each SKU.

Attach product images and notes.

Edit item details, with a record of who changed what and when.

Mark items as active, inactive or discontinued instead of deleting them, so history is kept.

Search and filter items by SKU, name, category, supplier or status.

Import items in bulk from a spreadsheet and export the item list.

16  Hierarchical Smart SKU Generator & Syntax Configurator

Description
A rule-based configuration engine that automatically generates standardized, human-readable Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) strings by encoding core product attributes (such as category, brand, variant, and supplier code) according to a predefined organizational syntax.

Purpose
To eliminate manual SKU creation errors, enforce strict cataloging consistency across large inventories, and allow staff and systems to instantly decipher basic product attributes directly from the SKU string without needing a database lookup.

 Main Functions/Activities
- **Attribute-to-Segment Mapping:** Configures custom segments (e.g., [Category]-[Brand]-[Color]-[Size]) to build uniform SKU patterns.
- **Automated Collision Prevention:** Automatically checks the database for existing codes to prevent duplicate or overlapping SKU generation.
- **Bulk Variant Matrix Generation:** Generates multi-variant SKUs simultaneously for items that come in multiple sizes, colors, or materials.
- **Prefix and Delimiter Customization:** Allows store managers to set standard separators (hyphens, dots, underscores) and category prefix codes.
